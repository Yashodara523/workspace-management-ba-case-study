# Service Station 360
### Business Analysis Case Study — Premium Coworking & Shared Office Platform

## Project Overview

Service Station 360 is a proposed digital platform for a premium coworking and shared-office business.

The case study explores how a centralised platform could improve workspace reservations, meeting-room management, catering operations and front-desk visibility.

The analysis focuses on translating operational challenges into clear business and system requirements.

> **Project type:** Business Analysis Case Study  
> **Approach:** Agile / User Stories  
> **Status:** Requirements analysis and solution proposal  
> **Implementation:** This repository documents the analysis and proposed requirements; it is not a deployed application.

## Business Problems

The scenario identifies three key operational challenges:

- Meeting rooms can be double-booked.
- Corporate catering orders can be lost or delayed.
- Front-desk employees have difficulty tracking occupancy and desk availability.

## Business Analysis Activities

### 1. Requirements Analysis

- Identified the main system capabilities.
- Developed 20 Agile user stories.
- Defined three acceptance criteria for each user story.
- Considered member, staff and administrator requirements.

### 2. Scope Change Management

Analysed a proposed smart-door integration and automated no-show policy.

The analysis covered:

- Requirement clarification.
- Technical dependencies.
- Schedule, cost and resource implications.
- Change control and stakeholder approval.
- Business communication with a non-technical stakeholder.

### 3. Stakeholder Requirements Gathering

Prepared a 30-minute requirements meeting agenda for the Head Community Manager.

Developed 10 probing questions covering:

- Live occupancy definitions.
- Data accuracy and refresh frequency.
- Meeting-room information.
- Catering order statuses.
- Dashboard permissions.
- Operational alerts and priorities.

### 4. Process Modelling

Prepared a process flowchart illustrating the proposed meeting-room no-show and automatic cancellation process.

### 5. User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

Analysed a cross-time-zone booking display defect.

Prepared:

- Defect reproduction steps.
- Expected and actual results.
- Business impact assessment.
- Investigation approach.
- Jira-style bug report.
- Acceptance criteria for the fix.
